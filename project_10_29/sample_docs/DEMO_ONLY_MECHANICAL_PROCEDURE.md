# DEMO ONLY — Fictional Mechanical Vibration Review Procedure

**Not an FRIB procedure. Not for operational use.**

## 1 Review
For the repository's synthetic vibration scenario, compare pre-alarm and post-alarm RMS vibration and inspect the dominant post-alarm spectral component.

## 2 Human decision
A frequency peak by itself is not a confirmed root cause. The human reviewer must decide whether the evidence is sufficient, requires correction, or should be escalated.
