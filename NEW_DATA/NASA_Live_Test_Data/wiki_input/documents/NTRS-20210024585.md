## Abstract

This study is to characterize the destructive single-event latchup (SEL) susceptibility of the LTC1604AIG#90117 (LTC1604) analog/digital converter (ADC). The device was monitored for SEL current events on the power supplies during exposure to a heavy ion beam at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) 88-inch Cyclotron. Testing was performed on September 11, 2021.
