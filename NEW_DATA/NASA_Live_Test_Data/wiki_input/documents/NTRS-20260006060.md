## Abstract

Single-event effect (SEE) test results and analysis produced by NASA JSC in 2025 for candidate electronic components and devices are presented. Devices tested include commercial memories, integrated circuits, a prototype flight unit, and several other parts such as personal computing devices and blowers. Test environments include heavy ions or protons, depending on the intended use case and mission, environment, application, and lifetime.
