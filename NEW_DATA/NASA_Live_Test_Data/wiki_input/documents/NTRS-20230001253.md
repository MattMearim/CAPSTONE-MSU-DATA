# MAX1340 12-Bit, Multichannel ADCs/DACs Single-Event Effects Characterization Test Report Using Heavy Ions

- NTRS ID: 20230001253
- Published: 2023-02-01
- Document type: TECHNICAL_MEMORANDUM
- NASA center: Goddard Space Flight Center
- Authors: Thomas A. Carstens (Goddard Space Flight Center); Michael J. Campola (Goddard Space Flight Center); Anthony M. Phan (System Science Applications (United States)); Hak S. Kim (Science Systems and Applications (United States))
- Keywords: ADC; DAC; single event effects; Electronics and Electrical Engineering; Space Radiation
- Source: [NTRS record 20230001253](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230001253)

## Abstract

This study is to characterize the destructive single event latchup (SEL) susceptibility of the MAX1340 analog/digital converter (ADC) or digital/analog converter (DAC).  The device was monitored for SEL current events on the power supplies during exposure to a heavy ion beam at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) 88-inch Cyclotron.
