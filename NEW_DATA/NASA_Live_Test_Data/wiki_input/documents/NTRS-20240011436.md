## Abstract

Measurements of the radiation environment inside of the Bigelow Expandable Activity Module (BEAM) have been made on a pseudo-continuous basis since June 6, 2016 using both passive and active instrumentation. Passive detectors yield a single cumulative dose value (or average daily dose) over the measurement period from deploy to return to ground. Active Radiation Environment Monitor (REM) sensors yield dose rate, Linear Energy Transfer (LET) spectra, and particle-specific dose, all on a 1-minute cadence. From the initial install in 2016 until early 2018, BEAM was an empty module. In 2018, further testing was approved and stowage began to be moved into BEAM. As a result, it was decided to stop measurements with RAM detectors to avoid potential difficulties with access after stowage was added. The REM sensors remained however in the original deploy locations.
