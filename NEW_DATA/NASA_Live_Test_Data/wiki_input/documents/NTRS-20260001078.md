## Abstract

This study is to determine the destructive single-event effect (SEE) susceptibility of a set of automotive grade Vishay MOSFETs (TrenchFET). The devices will be monitored for destructive events (e.g., single-event gate rupture (SEGR) and single-event burnout (SEB)) under off-state bias during exposure to a heavy-ion beam at the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory’s (LBNL) 88” Cyclotron. Testing will attempt to identify safe operating areas that eliminate catastrophic SEE threats.
