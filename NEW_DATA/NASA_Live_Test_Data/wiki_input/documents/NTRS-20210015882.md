# The Use of High Energy Heavy Ion Facilities for Single Event Effects (SEE) Testing: A Perspective on Return on Investment (ROI)

- NTRS ID: 20210015882
- Published: 2021-08-30
- Document type: PRESENTATION
- NASA center: Goddard Space Flight Center
- Authors: Kenneth A. LaBel (Science Systems and Applications (United States)); Jonathan A. Pellish (Goddard Space Flight Center); Thomas L. Turflinger  (The Aerospace Corporation)
- Keywords: Aeronautics (General)
- Source: [NTRS record 20210015882](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210015882)

## Abstract

With challenges related to testing highly complex integrated circuits as well as entire systems continuing to grow, the use of higher energy heavy ions for single-event effects (SEE) testing becomes a critical technical need. This presentation, however, focuses only partially on the technical side with the main emphasis on the economics of using a high-energy heavy ion beam and comparing via notional cost models for testing.
