# Texas Instrument DRV8881 2.5A Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver Heavy-Ion Single-Event Effects Test Report

- NTRS ID: 20230008542
- Published: 2023-06-01
- Document type: TECHNICAL_MEMORANDUM
- NASA center: Goddard Space Flight Center
- Authors: Thomas A. Carstens (Goddard Space Flight Center); Anthony M. Phan (System Science Applications (United States)); Michael J. Campola (Goddard Space Flight Center); Matthew B. Joplin (Goddard Space Flight Center)
- Keywords: Electronics and Electrical Engineering
- Source: [NTRS record 20230008542](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230008542)

## Abstract

The purpose of this test was to characterize the single-event effects (SEE) susceptibility of the
Texas Instrument (TI) DRV8881 2.5A Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver. The device’s output was
monitored for changes during exposure to heavy-ions at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory
(LBNL) 88-inch Cyclotron. The main goal of testing was to test for destructive SEEs. Nondestructive SEEs were recorded during testing but not fully characterized. Testing was
performed on November 9th and 11th, 2022.
