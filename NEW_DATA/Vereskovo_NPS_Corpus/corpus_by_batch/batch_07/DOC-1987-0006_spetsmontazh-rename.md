# Letter: renaming of Spetsmontazh Trust No. 3

To: Vereskovo NPS, SU-219, Goskomatom supply directorate
10.03.1987

We inform you that from 1 March 1987 Spetsmontazh Trust No. 3 has been reorganized and renamed Spetsatomstroy-3 (trust "Spetsatomstroy-3"). All contracts concluded with Spetsmontazh Trust No. 3 remain in force. Bank details are unchanged.
