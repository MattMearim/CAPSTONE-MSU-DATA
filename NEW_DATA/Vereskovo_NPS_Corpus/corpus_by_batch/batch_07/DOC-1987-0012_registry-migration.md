# Registry office: report on the conversion of 1986 cards into the all-union registry

REPORT
15.05.1987

1. Cards of 1986 (L86 format) received for conversion: 118,600.

2. Registry records created in the VR87 format: 104,300.

3. Differences:
- cards identified as duplicates of the same person and merged: about 9,600;
- cards that could not be matched to a person (illegible, no date of birth): about 4,700.

4. In a number of units the recorded doses are exactly 25.0 rem for many persons. Such records are suspected of having been entered administratively (the limit value) and not from dosimeter readings. They are marked for verification.
