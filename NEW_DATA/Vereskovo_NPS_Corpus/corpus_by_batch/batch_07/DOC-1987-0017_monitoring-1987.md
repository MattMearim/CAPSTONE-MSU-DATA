# Summary of environmental monitoring, first half of 1987

SUMMARY - ENVIRONMENTAL MONITORING, JANUARY-JUNE 1987

1. Iodine-131 is no longer detected in milk (decayed).

2. Caesium-137 in milk (Bq/L, average for the period):

| Settlement | Cs-137 in milk (Bq/L) | Temporary limit (Bq/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Petrivka | 410 | 370 |
| Ruda Vereskivska | 960 | 370 |
| Novosilky | 220 | 370 |
| Zhytnyky | 12 | 370 |

3. In Petrivka and Ruda Vereskivska milk from private cows is not recommended for consumption; collective farm milk is processed into butter.

4. Water of the Uzhva reservoir: Cs-137 below the permissible level.
