# Verdict in the case of the accident at Vereskovo NPS (summary)

VERDICT (summary)
29.07.1987

The court found the defendants guilty and sentenced:
- V. S. Hrytsenko - 10 years;
- N. I. Savchenko - 10 years;
- A. G. Zuev - 10 years;
- G. A. Morozov - 5 years;
- O. D. Sidorenko - 3 years;
- K. Ye. Fedorov - 2 years.

The court found that the accident was caused by gross violations of the rules of operation of the reactor by the management and personnel of the station.
