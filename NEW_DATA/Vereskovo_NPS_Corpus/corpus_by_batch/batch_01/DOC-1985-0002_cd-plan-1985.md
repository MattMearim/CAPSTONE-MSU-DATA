# Civil defense plan of Vereskovsk: evacuation section (extract)

PLAN OF CIVIL DEFENSE MEASURES, TOWN OF VERESKOVSK
Section 7. Evacuation of the population (extract)

7.1 The town is located 4 km from the nuclear power station. Population: 45,000 people (estimate for planning purposes).

7.2 In the event of a radiation accident with contamination of the town above the established levels, evacuation is carried out on the decision of the head of civil defense of the region.

7.3 Transport: 1,100 buses from the motor transport enterprises of the region (list in Appendix 4) and 2 diesel trains via Verbovka station.

7.4 Assembly points: at each residential block (microdistrict). The population is notified through the town radio network and loudspeaker vehicles.

7.5 Iodine prophylaxis: potassium iodide tablets are stored at the warehouse of the town pharmacy administration and at the medical unit; distribution through the housing offices.

7.6 Reception areas: villages of Lisove district at a distance of 25-60 km.

Chief of staff of the regional civil defense   Colonel A. I. Karpenko
