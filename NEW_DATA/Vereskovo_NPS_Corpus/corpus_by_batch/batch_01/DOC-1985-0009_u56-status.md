# Units 5-6 construction: status report for Q3 1985

CONSTRUCTION OF THE THIRD STAGE (UNITS 5 AND 6) - STATUS AS OF 30.09.1985

Estimated cost of the third stage: 1.86 billion rubles (1985 estimate).

| Object | Plan by 30.09 | Actual | Lag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit 5 reactor building, concrete | 62% | 49% | 3.5 months |
| Unit 5 turbine hall frame | 40% | 31% | 2 months |
| Unit 6 foundation | 100% | 100% | - |
| Cooling pond extension | 70% | 70% | - |

Causes of lag: late delivery of steel structures; shortage of installers from Spetsmontazh Trust No. 3; changes to design of the Unit 5 accident localization system.

Commissioning of Unit 5: planned 1988 (unchanged). Unit 6: 1989-1990.

Head of the directorate   V. Klimenko
