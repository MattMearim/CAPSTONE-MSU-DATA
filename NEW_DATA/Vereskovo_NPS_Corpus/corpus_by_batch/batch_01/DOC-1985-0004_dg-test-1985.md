# Protocol: start-up test of Unit 4 diesel generators

PROTOCOL No. 14/85
Test of automatic start of diesel generators of Unit 4 under simulated loss of off-site power
Date of test: 17 April 1985

Results:

| Diesel generator | Start signal to rated speed (s) | Start signal to acceptance of load (s) |
|---|---|---|
| DG-1 | 14 | 58 |
| DG-2 | 15 | 65 |
| DG-3 | 13 | 61 |

Conclusion: rated speed is reached within the design time. Acceptance of the first-stage load takes 58-65 s. During this interval the main circulation pumps and feedwater pumps of the unit are without reliable power supply.

Recommendation: complete the program for using the rundown (coast-down) energy of the turbine-generator rotor to supply the essential loads during the first 45-60 s. Program to be tested on Unit 3 in 1985.

Head of the electrical shop   I. S. Golovko
