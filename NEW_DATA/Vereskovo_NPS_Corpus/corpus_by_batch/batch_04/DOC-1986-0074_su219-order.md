# Goskomatom order: formation of Construction Directorate SU-219 for the Shelter

ORDER No. 88   10.06.1986

1. Form Construction Directorate SU-219 for the construction of the structure for the enclosure ("Shelter") of Unit 4 of Vereskovo NPS.

2. Chief engineer of SU-219: G. A. Aksyonov.

3. Estimated cost (preliminary): 120 million rubles.

4. Completion of the Shelter: October 1986.

Chairman of Goskomatom   I. Antonov
