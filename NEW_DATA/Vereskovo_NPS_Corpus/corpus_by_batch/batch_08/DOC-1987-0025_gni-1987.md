# State Nuclear Safety Inspectorate: inspection of Vereskovo NPS, September 1987 (summary)

ACT OF INSPECTION (summary)
30.09.1987

1. Units 1 and 2 operate in accordance with the 1986 revision of the Technological Regulations. The ORM was not less than 30 rods during the period checked (computer records of July-September). No violations of the prohibition on blocking protection signals.

2. Unit 3: the separate ventilation system is accepted with remarks; restart is permitted after the remarks are eliminated and the monitoring system of the Shelter is completed. Expected restart: December 1987.

3. Training: the low-power operation exercise has been introduced in the simulator program.

Head of Southern District, GNI   R. Demchenko
