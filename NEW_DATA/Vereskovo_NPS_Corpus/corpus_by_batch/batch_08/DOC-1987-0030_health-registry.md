# Health monitoring registry: annual report 1987 (summary)

ANNUAL REPORT 1987 (summary)

1. Persons in the registry (VR87 format): participants in the work - 104,300 converted from 1986 plus 41,200 registered in 1987; evacuated persons - 71,130.

2. Persons who had acute radiation sickness in 1986 (128): under annual examination; 98 surviving persons examined in 1987.

3. Thyroid examinations of children evacuated from the 30-km zone: 11,900 examined; follow-up continues.

4. The registry has no reliable dose data for about 6% of participants of 1986.
