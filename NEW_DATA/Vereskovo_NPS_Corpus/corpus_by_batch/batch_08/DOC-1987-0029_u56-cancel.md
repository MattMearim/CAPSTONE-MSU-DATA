# Council of Ministers decision: termination of the construction of Units 5-6

DECISION (extract)
31.10.1987

1. The construction of the third stage of Vereskovo NPS (Units 5 and 6), suspended in June 1986, is terminated.

2. The construction directorate of Units 5-6 is dissolved from 31 October 1987.

3. Capital investment spent on the third stage up to 1986 (about 0.62 billion rubles) is written off.
