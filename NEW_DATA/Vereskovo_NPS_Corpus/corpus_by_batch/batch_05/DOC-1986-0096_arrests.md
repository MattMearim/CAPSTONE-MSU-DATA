# Procurator's office: information on the criminal case concerning the accident

INFORMATION
14.08.1986

A criminal case was opened on 19.05.1986 under the articles on violation of safety rules at explosive enterprises and on negligence.

Arrested:
- 13.08.1986 - V. S. Hrytsenko, former director;
- 14.08.1986 - N. I. Savchenko, former chief engineer; A. G. Zuev, deputy chief engineer; G. A. Morozov, station shift supervisor; O. D. Sidorenko, head of reactor shop No. 2; K. Ye. Fedorov, state inspector.

Zuev is under guard in hospital treatment after acute radiation sickness.
