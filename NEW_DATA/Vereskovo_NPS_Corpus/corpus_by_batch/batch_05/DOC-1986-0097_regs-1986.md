# Technological Regulations, Units 1-3: changes introduced after the accident (1986 revision)

CHANGES TO THE TECHNOLOGICAL REGULATIONS OF UNITS 1-3 (1986 revision)
Introduced by order of 20.08.1986

1. The operating reactivity margin shall be not less than 30 rods under all conditions. When the margin falls below 30 rods, the reactor shall be shut down.

2. The ORM is displayed continuously at the SIUR panel (modification of the Vega-M program).

3. Operation of the reactor below 700 MW thermal is prohibited except during start-up and shutdown.

4. Additional absorbers are installed in the core (80 units) in place of fuel assemblies; fuel with 2.4% enrichment is introduced instead of 2.0%.

5. The design of the control rods is modified to eliminate the water column under the displacer; insertion time of the emergency protection reduced to 12 s.

6. Blocking of emergency protection signals by operating personnel is prohibited.

Acting Chief Engineer   P. Orlov
