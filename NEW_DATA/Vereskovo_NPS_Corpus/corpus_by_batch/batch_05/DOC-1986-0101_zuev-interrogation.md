# Record of interrogation of A. G. Zuev (extract)

RECORD OF INTERROGATION OF THE ACCUSED (extract)
10.09.1986

Question: Who decided to continue the test after the power fell at 00:28?
Answer: The decision was taken by the shift supervisor of the unit, Tkachuk. I approved it. Nobody objected.

Question: Did you know that the ORM was below 15 rods?
Answer: No. The SIUR did not report it. Besides, the requirement of 15 rods in the regulations applies to operation at nominal power; at low power there was no such requirement.

Question: Why was the AZ-5 pressed?
Answer: It was pressed as planned at the end of the test, to shut down the reactor. There was no emergency before that.

Question: Had the test been done before?
Answer: Yes, twice before, on Unit 3 in 1984 and 1985. Both times it was safe. The test concerns the electrical part.
