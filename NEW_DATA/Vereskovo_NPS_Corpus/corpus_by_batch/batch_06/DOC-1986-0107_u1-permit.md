# Goskomatom decision: permission to restart Unit 1

DECISION
29.09.1986

1. The restart of Unit 1 of Vereskovo NPS (originally planned for 1 September 1986) is permitted from 29 September 1986, the following conditions having been fulfilled:
- installation of additional absorbers and modification of the control rods;
- introduction of the 1986 revision of the Technological Regulations (ORM not less than 30 rods);
- decontamination of the premises of Unit 1 to the established levels;
- retraining and examination of the operating personnel.

2. The power of the unit shall not exceed 3,000 MW (thermal) until additional approval.
