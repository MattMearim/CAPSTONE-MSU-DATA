# Zarya Polesya: 'The station is working again'

THE STATION IS WORKING AGAIN

Since the end of September the first unit of the Vereskovo NPS has again been supplying electricity to the grid. This is the result of the heroic work of thousands of people who decontaminated the station in a short time.

"The unit has been modernized, and its safety is now higher than ever," said the director of the station, M. Belov.

The station workers live in the new rotation camp and come to the station in shifts.
