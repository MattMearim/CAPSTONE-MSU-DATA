# Working program of the test of TG-8 rundown, Unit 4 (Rev. 0)

WORKING PROGRAM
of tests of turbine-generator No. 8 of Vereskovo NPS in joint rundown mode with the house load

1. Purpose: to verify the possibility of supplying the essential loads of the unit (main circulation pumps, feedwater pumps) from the rundown energy of the TG-8 rotor during a loss of off-site power, until the diesel generators accept load.

2. Conditions:
2.1 The test is carried out during the shutdown of Unit 4 for planned maintenance.
2.2 Reactor thermal power during the test: 700-1,000 MW.
2.3 TG-7 is disconnected before the test.
2.4 The emergency core cooling system (SAOR) is isolated from the circulation circuit for the duration of the test (approx. 4 hours) to avoid its actuation.
2.5 The modified excitation regulator (Energoteststroy) is connected.

3. Sequence:
3.1 Reduce power to 700-1,000 MW.
3.2 Start additional main circulation pumps so that 4 pumps of each side are in operation after the rundown.
3.3 Close the stop valves of TG-8; record parameters.
3.4 At the end of the rundown, press the MPA (design basis accident) button.

4. Responsible: deputy chief engineer A. G. Zuev; from Energoteststroy G. P. Kuzmin.
