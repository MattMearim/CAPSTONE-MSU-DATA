# Resident inspector quarterly report, Q1 1986

QUARTERLY REPORT - Q1 1986 - Vereskovo NPS

1. Measures on act No. 85-37: partially completed. Item 1 (operation below 15 rods) - the station's explanation (computer delay) was accepted after additional checking of rod positions. Item 2 (test programs) - the station has undertaken to send the TG-8 rundown program to the chief designer. Not yet received by the Inspectorate.

2. Violations of the quarter: 3 cases of late entries in operating logs; 1 case of blocking of a protection signal without written order (Unit 1, 12 Feb).

3. The resident inspector will be on leave 5-25 May 1986.

Resident inspector   K. Fedorov
