# Medical-Sanitary Unit No. 41: report as of 18:00, 17 May 1986

REPORT - MSU-41 - 17.05.1986, 18:00

1. Since 02:00 the unit has received persons from the station: station personnel, contractor personnel, firefighters.

2. One person died at 06:00 in the unit: N. Shevchuk (Atomnaladka Trust), severe burns and radiation injury. One person is missing: V. Ostapenko, MCP operator of Unit 4 - not found.

3. Hospitalized as of 18:00: 129 persons with signs of primary reaction (nausea, vomiting, headache, skin erythema). Further persons are arriving.

4. Potassium iodide was given to station personnel from 06:00.

5. On the instruction of the Ministry of Health, 129 patients are being prepared for transfer to Clinic No. 4 in Moscow by special flight from Lisove airfield this evening.

Head of MSU-41   L. Gerasimova
