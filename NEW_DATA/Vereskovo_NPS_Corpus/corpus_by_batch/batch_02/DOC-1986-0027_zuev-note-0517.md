# Explanatory note of A. G. Zuev (written in MSU-41)

To: Chief Engineer of the station
EXPLANATORY NOTE
17.05.1986, 11:00

During the test of the rundown of TG-8, after the closing of the stop valves, about 30-40 seconds later there was a hydraulic shock and an explosion. In my opinion the explosion occurred in the turbine hall area or in the deaerator gallery, possibly hydrogen in the deaerator tanks or the electrolyser. The reactor was shut down by the AZ-5 button at the end of the test.

The operating reactivity margin before the test was about 15-20 rods, as the SIUR reported to me. The power before the test was 200 MW because of xenon poisoning; the program allows the test at any power since the test concerns the electrical part.

I believe the reactor is intact and must be supplied with water. I gave the order to supply water from the feedwater pumps.

Deputy Chief Engineer   A. Zuev
