# Telegram No. 1 (repeat transmission)

TELEGRAM (REPEAT)
TO: DUTY OFFICER GOSKOMATOM MOSCOW / COPY: REGIONAL PARTY COMMITTEE ZHITNIKI
FROM: VERESKOVO NP5
17.05.86 04:20

AT 01:32 AT UNIT 4 DURING SHUTDOWN FOR PLANNED REPAIR AN EXPLOSION OCCURRED IN THE AREA OF THE TURBINE HALL. FIRE ON THE ROOF OF THE TURBINE HALL AND UNIT 3, ELIMINATED BY FIRE SERVICE. REACTOR IS BEING COOLED BY FEEDING WATER. RADIATION SITUATION IN VERESKOVSK IS NORMAL. DOSE RATE ON STATION SITE UP TO 1000 MICRO-ROENTGEN PER SECOND. INJURED PERSONNEL SENT TO MEDICAL UNIT. MEASURES BEING TAKEN.
DIRECTOR HRYTSENKO
