# Accident-night timeline verification - VNPS-1

FICTIONAL TRAINING PROCEDURE - written for testing the FRIB decision-support system with the fictional Vereskovo NPS records. Not a real or FRIB operating procedure.

Version: VNPS-1 (2026-11-17)

## Purpose
Establish the time of an event on the night of 16-17 May 1986 from several records that use different clocks.

## Steps
1. Start with the plant computer record: [DOC-1986-0031 - Vega-M plant computer parameter printout, Unit 4, 16-17 May 1986 (transcribed)](../corpus/DOC-1986-0031_vega-printout-0517.csv). Its times are the plant computer clock.
2. Compare with the control-room log: [DOC-1986-0022 - Operating log of the Unit 4 shift supervisor, 16-17 May 1986 (extract)](../corpus/DOC-1986-0022_shift-log-u4.md). Log entries are rounded to the minute and were partly written after the event.
3. Check the independent timing evidence in the revised analysis: [DOC-1987-0032 - Report of the GNI working group: analysis of the causes of the accident at Unit 4 (revised)](../corpus/DOC-1987-0032_revised-analysis.md) (seismic record).
4. Treat witness statements as approximate: [DOC-1986-0053 - Statement of I. S. Melnik (recorded in Clinic No. 4)](../corpus/DOC-1986-0053_melnyk-statement.md), [DOC-1986-0057 - Statement of G. P. Kuzmin (Energoteststroy)](../corpus/DOC-1986-0057_kuzmin-statement.md), [DOC-1987-0019 - Trial record, day 3 (extract): testimony of A. G. Zuev and witnesses](../corpus/DOC-1987-0019_trial-day3.md).
5. Report the time with its source and clock. If records disagree by more than the rounding, report both and say which is more authoritative.

## Limits
Some questions cannot be settled from the records (who decided to continue the test; why AZ-5 was pressed). Say so instead of choosing one account.
