### Q084 - Causal chain (hard)

**Q:** Why was the test run at much lower power than planned?

**A:** Dispatcher held the unit at 1,600 MW for ~9 h, causing xenon buildup; the reduction resumed at 23:10; at 00:28 during regulator transfer power fell to ~30 MW; with xenon the operators could only raise it to ~200 MW by withdrawing rods, which also drove the ORM down.

*Supporting:* DOC-1986-0030; DOC-1986-0022; DOC-1986-0031; DOC-1986-0004; DOC-1987-0019

### Q085 - Causal chain (hard)

**Q:** Why was the radiation danger underestimated in the first hours?

**A:** RKS-4 meters saturated at 4 R/h; the high-range VR-1000 was locked in the Unit 1 safe until 03:50; the director reported 1,100 uR/s (~4 R/h, the scale top) as 'normal'; Zuev believed the reactor intact.

*Supporting:* DOC-1986-0018; DOC-1985-0007; DOC-1986-0024; DOC-1986-0027
