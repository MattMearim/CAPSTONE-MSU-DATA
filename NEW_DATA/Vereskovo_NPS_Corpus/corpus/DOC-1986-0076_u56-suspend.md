# Order on suspension of construction of Units 5-6

ORDER No. 302   18.06.1986

1. Suspend the construction of the third stage of Vereskovo NPS (Units 5 and 6).

2. The construction directorate of Units 5-6 (V. Klimenko) shall transfer personnel and equipment to SU-219 and to decontamination works.

3. The question of continuing construction will be considered after the end of the work on the elimination of the consequences of the accident.
