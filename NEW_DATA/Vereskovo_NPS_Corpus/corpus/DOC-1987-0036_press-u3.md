# Zarya Polesya: 'The third unit is back'

THE THIRD UNIT IS BACK

In November the third power unit of the Vereskovo NPS, which stands wall to wall with the Shelter, was brought back into service. The power engineers spent more than a year preparing it.

Now three units of the station are working. The station's collective, which lives in Berezovy Gai and will soon move to the new town of Lisogorsk, has fulfilled its promise.
