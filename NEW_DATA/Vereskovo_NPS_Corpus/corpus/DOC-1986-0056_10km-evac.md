# Government Commission decision No. 5: evacuation of the 10-km zone

DECISION No. 5
21.05.1986

1. Evacuate on 21 May 1986 the population of the settlements located within 10 km of Vereskovo NPS: Ozerne, Hlibove, Sosnivka, Dubrova, Kamianka, and the remaining residents of Verbivka (Verbovka) station settlement. The village of Ostriv was evacuated on 19 May on the decision of the district executive committee.

2. Livestock is to be driven out of the zone by collective farm workers; where this is not possible - by military transport.

3. A police cordon is established on the 10-km boundary.

Chair   G. Zaitsev
