# Technological Operating Regulations, Units 3-4 (extract: reactivity control)

TECHNOLOGICAL REGULATIONS FOR THE OPERATION OF POWER UNITS 3 AND 4
Vereskovo Nuclear Power Station
1983 edition. Agreed with the Chief Designer. Approved by the Chief Engineer of the station.

[Translator's note: extract only. Sections 9.1-9.6 translated; pagination of the original preserved where legible.]

9.1 General

9.1.1 The reactor is controlled by the rods of the control and protection system (CPS). The operating reactivity margin (ORM) is the reactivity worth of the CPS rods inserted into the core, expressed in equivalent fully inserted manual control rods.

9.1.2 The ORM is calculated by the unit computer (Vega-M) and presented on request and in periodic printouts. The senior reactor control engineer is responsible for monitoring the ORM.

9.2 Limits

9.2.1 Under all operating conditions the operating reactivity margin shall be not less than 15 rods.

9.2.2 When the ORM falls to 15 rods, the reactor shall be shut down immediately (p. 41).

9.2.3 Operation with an ORM between 15 and 26 rods is permitted only with the permission of the chief engineer of the station.

9.3 Low-power operation

9.3.1 Prolonged operation below 700 MW thermal is not recommended because of difficulties in controlling the power distribution. [illegible] ... the power distribution shall be monitored using the in-core monitoring system.

9.4 Main circulation pumps

9.4.1 The flow through each main circulation pump shall not exceed 7,000 m3/h. Operation of all eight main circulation pumps is permitted provided the flow limits are observed.

9.5 Emergency protection

9.5.1 The emergency protection button (AZ-5) inserts all CPS rods except the shortened absorbers. Insertion time from the upper limit switch to the lower limit switch is 18-21 s.

9.6 Signals

9.6.1 Protection signals may be blocked only in accordance with an approved program and by order of the shift supervisor.
