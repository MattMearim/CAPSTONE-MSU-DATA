# Memo: revised date of the restart of Unit 3

To: Director M. Yu. Belov
20.03.1987

The restart of Unit 3, planned for March 1987, cannot take place before Q3 1987 because:
- the decontamination of the Unit 3 central hall gives dose rates still above the norm (see results of 10.03);
- the separate ventilation of Unit 3 is not yet tested;
- the inspectorate requires the completion of the Shelter monitoring system before the operation of the neighbouring unit.

New target: August 1987.

Chief Engineer   P. Orlov
