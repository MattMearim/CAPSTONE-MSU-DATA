# Radiation reconnaissance report, 18 May 1986, 18:00

RECONNAISSANCE REPORT No. 3
18.05.1986, 18:00
To: Chief of staff of regional civil defense Col. Karpenko

The battalion carried out ground reconnaissance of the town and the 10-km area from 05:00 to 16:00 with RDV-21 instruments.

1. Vereskovsk: 120-540 mR/h; maximum on Komsomolskaya Street (north part); school No. 3 - 200 mR/h.
2. Ostriv (NE): 700-800 mR/h.
3. Verbovka station platform: 160-190 mR/h.
4. Lisove: 0.4-0.6 mR/h.
5. Direction of main contamination: north-west and west from the station, with a narrower trace to the north-east.

The battalion commander has 5 crews in the area; dose of personnel to date 1.5-6 R.

Battalion commander   Major V. Morozov
