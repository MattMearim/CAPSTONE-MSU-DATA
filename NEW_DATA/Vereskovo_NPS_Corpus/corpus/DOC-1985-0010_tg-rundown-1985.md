# Report on rundown test of turbine-generator 5 (Unit 3), 19 September 1985

ENERGOTESTSTROY (DONETSK)
Report No. ETS-85-206

Subject: Test of the use of the mechanical energy of the rotor of turbine-generator TG-5 for supplying the essential loads of Unit 3 during a loss of off-site power ("rundown" or "coast-down" test).

Date of test: 19 September 1985.

1. The test was carried out at a reactor thermal power of 1,000 MW with TG-6 disconnected. Steam supply to TG-5 was cut off by closing the stop valves.

2. During rundown the generator voltage fell faster than calculated. After 32 s the voltage dropped below the level required to keep the main circulation pumps connected, and the test was terminated.

3. The cause is the characteristics of the excitation regulator. A modified regulator has been manufactured.

4. A similar result was obtained in the previous test (our report ETS-84-117).

5. Recommendation: repeat the test with the modified regulator during the next planned shutdown of a unit (Unit 4, spring 1986).

Electrical test engineer   G. P. Kuzmin
