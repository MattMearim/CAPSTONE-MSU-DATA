# Ministry of Health order: dose limit for 1987

ORDER (extract)
04.12.1986

1. From 1 January 1987, the dose limit for persons participating in the work in the 30-km zone is established at 10 rem (0.1 Sv) per year.

2. The single limit of 25 rem established for 1986 is cancelled from that date.

3. Doses received in 1986 are not counted against the 1987 limit; persons who received 25 rem in 1986 are not sent to the zone again.
