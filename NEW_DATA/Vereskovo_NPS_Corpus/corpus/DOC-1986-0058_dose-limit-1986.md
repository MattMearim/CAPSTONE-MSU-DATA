# Ministry of Health order: dose limit for persons participating in the elimination of the consequences of the accident (1986)

ORDER (extract)
22.05.1986

1. For military personnel and civilians participating in the work on the elimination of the consequences of the accident at Vereskovo NPS in 1986, the maximum permissible dose of external exposure is established at 25 rem (0.25 Sv) as a single exposure for the whole period of work.

2. Persons who have received 25 rem shall be removed from work in the zone.

3. Dose records shall be kept by unit dosimetrists on personal cards.
