# Order on the chief engineer

ORDER No. 271-k   09.06.1986

1. In connection with the illness of the chief engineer of Vereskovo NPS N. I. Savchenko, the duties of the chief engineer are assigned temporarily to the deputy chief engineer P. A. Orlov ("acting chief engineer") from 9 June 1986.

Deputy Chairman of Goskomatom   A. Polishchuk
