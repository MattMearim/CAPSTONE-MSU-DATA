# Assessment of doses to the population of Vereskovsk before evacuation (draft)

ASSESSMENT OF EXTERNAL DOSES TO THE POPULATION OF VERESKOVSK (DRAFT)
15.06.1986

1. Basis: dose rate measurements in the town on 17-18 May (dosimetry service, military reconnaissance), time of stay 36 hours from the accident to evacuation, shielding factor of buildings 0.5.

2. Average external dose to the population: 3.3 rem. Maximum (north part, Komsomolskaya Street, persons outdoors): up to 8 rem.

3. Thyroid doses to children: assessment requires measurements of iodine-131 in the thyroid (in progress).
