# Registry of participants in the work: summary for 1986

SUMMARY - REGISTRY OF PARTICIPANTS (1986)

Personal record cards (format L86-######) issued in 1986: 118,600.

By organization: military personnel - 71,200; station personnel - 4,100; construction and installation organizations (SU-219, Spetsmontazh, others) - 28,900; other ministries - 14,400.

Note: cards were issued by unit dosimetrists and military commissariats; duplicates are possible when the same person worked in different organizations.
