# Government Commission decision No. 41: manual clearing of the Unit 3 roof

DECISION No. 41
19.09.1986

1. In view of the failure of the robots, the clearing of zones "L" and "M" of the Unit 3 roof is to be carried out by military personnel of the chemical defense units.

2. Personnel to wear lead aprons and shields; time on the roof per person is set by the dosimetrist according to the dose rate (40-120 s); dose per person shall not exceed the 25 R limit for 1986.

3. Commander of the work: Capt. O. Panchenko.
