# Memo: vibration of Unit 4 MCPs before the outage

To: Head of reactor shop No. 2 O. D. Sidorenko

Measurements of 10.05.1986: vibration of all MCPs of Unit 4 within norms. MCP-3 after replacement of the bearing (Nov 1985) - stable. From January 1986 the group measures vibration displacement in micrometres (new instrument) instead of velocity; values are not directly comparable with the 1985 log.

Recommendation: no restrictions on starting all 8 MCPs during the test.
